Startup Thinking. In Tamil.

Real startup lessons, in the language you think in — not the one you had to learn business in.

Watch on YouTube See the framework ↓

Most startup advice online assumes you think in English. Tamil Founder Mind doesn't. Every video breaks down real business thinking — validation, pricing, hiring, negotiation — the way you'd explain it to a friend over filter coffee, not the way a pitch deck explains it to an investor.

Every founder's journey follows the same three phases. Most channels only cover the first one.

01

Build

Validate the idea before you spend a rupee on it. First customers, first version, first no's — and what to actually do with them.

02

Automate

The unglamorous systems that let a business run without you standing over it every hour — pricing logic, hiring checklists, the tools that do the repeating for you.

03

Sustain

Staying in business long enough to matter. Cash flow discipline, retention over acquisition, and the plans for the year nobody claps for.
